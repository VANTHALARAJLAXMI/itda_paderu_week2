Tribal Coffee Cultivation

Paderu Agency organic Arabica coffee plantations and tribal farmer economic empowerment.

Overview of Paderu Organic Coffee

Paderu Agency is nationally renowned for organic shade-grown Arabica coffee. Coffee plantations in Minimuluru, Chintapalli, and Araku offer sustainable livelihoods for thousands of tribal families, transforming podu (shifting) cultivation into eco-friendly economic prosperity.

Key Statistics & Institutional Support

    Nodal Office: Deputy Director (Extension), Coffee Board, Paderu. Key Plantations: Minimuluru, Ananthagiri, Araku Valley, Chintapalli. Shade Trees: Silver Oak, Pepper vines inter-cropped with coffee. Market Linkage: AP Girijan Cooperative Corporation (GCC) and tribal coffee marketing collectives.
