TRIBAL EDUCATION EXCELLENCE

Super 50 Coaching Programme

Specialized residential coaching and mentorship initiative for top tribal SSC students in Paderu Agency.

About Super 50 Initiative

The Super 50 initiative by ITDA Paderu identifies top-performing Class 10 (SSC) tribal students across Ashram schools and residential institutions in ASR District to provide intensive academic coaching, career guidance, and competitive exam preparation.

Performance & Success Highlights

Academic Year Results & Outcomes achieved
2023 – 24 51 students passed in 1st Division (21 Boys, 30 Girls). 2 students joined IIIT Nuzvid, 35 joined Colleges of Excellence.
2024 – 25 100% 1st Division pass rate (51 students). 3 joined IIIT Nuzvid, 41 joined Colleges of Excellence, 6 joined higher institutions.
2025 – 26 Screening completed on Sept 1, 2025. 104 merit students selected (51 Boys, 53 Girls) for advanced coaching.

Key Focus Areas

    Daily specialized subject classes in Mathematics, Science, and English. Mock test series and continuous evaluation by expert faculty. Residential accommodation with nutritional meals and health monitoring. Guidance for IIIT entrance, APRJC, and Colleges of Excellence.
