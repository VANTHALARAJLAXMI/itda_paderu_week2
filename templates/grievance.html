About Public Grievances

What is a Grievance? A formal representation or complaint by a citizen regarding delay, denial, or inadequacy of government services, school amenities, health facilities, or scheme benefits.

Types of Grievances Handled:

  • Ashram School amenities, food quality, or hostel maintenance
  • Primary Health Centre (PHC) staff presence or medicine availability
  • Coffee plantation sapling supply or seedling distribution
  • Rural road repairs, drinking water handpumps, or streetlight faults
  • Scholarship application status or pension disbursements

How Official Grievances are Processed:

1. Receipt at weekly ITDA Spandana / Grievance Day (Every Monday at ITDA Office Paderu).

2. Tagging to concerned Line Department Officer.

3. Field inspection and resolution tracking by Project Officer.
