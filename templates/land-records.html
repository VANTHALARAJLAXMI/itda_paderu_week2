Land Records & Scheduled Area Rights

Guidance on land ownership verification, 1/70 Land Transfer Regulation (LTR), and Forest Rights Act (FRA)

Land Transfer Regulation (1/70 Act)

Overview: In Scheduled Agency Areas, transfer of immovable property from a tribal to a non-tribal person is legally prohibited under Regulation 1/70 to protect indigenous tribal land rights.

Citizen Guidance: For official land mutation, survey verification, or title dispute verification, citizens must approach the Tahsildar Office or Sub-Collector / RDO Office, Paderu.

Forest Rights Act (RoFR Titles)

Overview: Recognition of Forest Rights Act (RoFR) grants traditional forest-dwelling tribal families title rights (patta) over traditional podu lands cultivated by them.

Where to Approach: Gram Sabha, District Level Committee (DLC) / ITDA Survey section.

📌 Official Verification Note: This portal does not display individual land survey numbers or private land ownership records. To view official webland records or apply for certified copies, visit the state Meeseva / Revenue portal or the Tahsildar office.
