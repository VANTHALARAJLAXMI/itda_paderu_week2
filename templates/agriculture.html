Agriculture & Coffee Plantation

Tribal farmer welfare, Minimuluru coffee cultivation, horticultural crops, and organic agriculture support

Key Regional Speciality

Minimuluru & Agency Tribal Coffee Cultivation

The Paderu Agency region in the Eastern Ghats is renowned for organic tribal coffee cultivation. Minimuluru area coffee plantations are an important socio-economic feature.

ITDA Paderu works closely with the Coffee Board (Deputy Director Extension, Paderu) and AP Markfed to supply coffee saplings, shade tree nursery stock, processing equipment, and marketing support to tribal coffee growers.

Tribal Farmer Support & Seeds Supply

Purpose: Promoting sustainable agricultural practices and food security for tribal farmers.

Services: Subsidized quality seeds, organic fertilizers, soil testing, and micro-irrigation equipment.

Beneficiaries: Tribal farmers owning or cultivating land in Scheduled Area mandals.

Where to Approach: Agriculture Officer / Horticulture Officer, ITDA Office Paderu, or local Rythu Bharosa Kendra (RBK).

Horticulture & Plantation Crops

Purpose: Diversifying income sources through high-value horticultural plantation crops.

Services: Pepper, turmeric, ginger, pineapple, and fruit orchard nursery distribution.

Beneficiaries: Small and marginal tribal cultivators in Agency area.

Where to Approach: Horticulture Officer, ITDA Paderu.
