Key Coordinated Departments

Multi-sector line departments operating under ITDA Paderu integrated development framework

Education

Description: Managing primary, secondary, and residential tribal education.

Health

Description: Government healthcare through District Hospital, Area Hospital and Community Health Centres in ASR District, including tribal and agency areas.

Responsibilities: Hospital services, maternal and child health programmes, blood services, newborn care, eye care and public-health systems listed on the district health page.

Tribal Welfare

Description: Scheduled Area administration, PVTG communities, welfare scheme categories, livelihoods and culture — without assigning every festival or dance to every tribe.
