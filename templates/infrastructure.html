Educational & Institutional Buildings

Managed by the Tribal Welfare Engineering Wing (TWEW). Responsible for constructing modern Ashram school buildings, additional classrooms, digital lab halls, post-matric college hostels, and Youth Training Centres (YTC).

Civic & Community Infrastructure

Covers drinking water overhead tanks, solar water pumps, staff quarters for teachers and healthcare workers in interior habitations, and community centers.
