Government Welfare Schemes

Overview of Tribal Welfare, Educational, Agricultural, and Social Security Schemes in Paderu Agency

Educational Scheme

Super 50 SSC Excellence Scheme

Purpose: Special residential coaching for high-scoring tribal Class 10 students.

Target Beneficiaries: ST SSC students in Agency Ashram and Government schools.

Benefits: Intensive coaching, free study material, IIIT entrance guidance.

Eligibility: Merit screening test score and academic record.

Application Process: Selected via annual district screening test.

Required Documents: School bonafide certificate, marksheet, caste certificate, Aadhaar.

Where to Apply: School Headmaster / ITDA Tribal Welfare Section, Paderu.

* Please verify current eligibility, benefits and application procedure from the concerned official department.

Hostel Scheme

Post-Matric Hostel Admission Scheme

Purpose: Free accommodation and boarding for tribal college students.

Target Beneficiaries: ST students pursuing Intermediate, ITI, Degree, and PG courses.

Benefits: Free lodging, mess facilities, and study environment.

Eligibility: Enrolled in recognized colleges in Paderu Agency area.

Application Process: Apply online/offline during hostel notification window.

Required Documents: College admission proof, caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar.

Where to Apply: Hostel Warden / ITDA Tribal Welfare Office.

* Please verify current eligibility, benefits and application procedure from the concerned official department.

Agricultural Scheme

Minimuluru Tribal Coffee Expansion Scheme

Purpose: Expanding organic coffee acreage and supporting tribal growers.

Target Beneficiaries: Tribal farmers cultivating agency lands.

Benefits: Coffee saplings, shade tree nursery stock, pulper machines, technical extension.

Eligibility: Registered tribal land cultivator in coffee-growing mandals.

Required Documents: Land ownership/RoFR title document, Aadhaar card, bank passbook.

Where to Apply: Deputy Director (Extension), Coffee Board, Paderu.

* Please verify current eligibility, benefits and application procedure from the concerned official department.

Livelihood Scheme

TRICOR Self-Employment Subsidy Scheme

Purpose: Providing financial assistance for tribal youth micro-enterprises.

Target Beneficiaries: Unemployed ST youth and tribal women SHGs.

Benefits: Capital subsidy paired with bank credit linkage.

Required Documents: Project proposal, caste certificate, income certificate, bank account details.

Where to Apply: Executive Director, TRICOR / MPDO office.

* Please verify current eligibility, benefits and application procedure from the concerned official department.

📌 Official Verification Notice: Scheme guidelines, annual financial allocations, eligibility criteria, and deadlines are subject to official government notifications. Always confirm live details at the ITDA Paderu office.
