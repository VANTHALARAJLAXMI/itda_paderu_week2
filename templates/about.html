About ITDA Paderu

Integrated Tribal Development Agency, Paderu — Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR) District, Andhra Pradesh

Agency Overview

The Integrated Tribal Development Agency (ITDA) Paderu is the government administrative body responsible for planning, implementing, and monitoring tribal welfare and development programmes in the Paderu Agency region.

Located at Paderu in Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR) District, ITDA coordinates multi-sectoral development covering education, healthcare, organic agriculture, coffee cultivation, infrastructure, and livelihoods.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To empower tribal communities through sustainable development, quality education, healthcare, and socio-economic equality while preserving cultural heritage.

Mission

Integrated execution of central and state welfare programmes, Ashram school management, healthcare extension, and tribal coffee plantation development.

Key Objectives & Responsibilities

  • Educational Advancement: Managing Ashram schools, post-matric hostels, and Super 50 coaching for tribal students.
  • Healthcare Services: Ensuring primary medical care, maternal health, and nutrition in remote agency habitations.
  • Agricultural Support: Promoting organic farming, coffee cultivation around Minimuluru, and farmer assistance.
  • Infrastructure Development: Building roads, bridges, school quarters, drinking water, and community structures.
  • Public Service Delivery: Redressing citizen grievances and facilitating welfare schemes.
