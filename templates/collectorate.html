District Collectorate

The administrative headquarters of Alluri Sitharama Raju District is at Paderu.

Collectorate, Paderu

The District Collectorate at Paderu is the seat of general administration for Alluri Sitharama Raju District. District-level coordination of revenue, law and order, disaster response and development programmes is organised from this office.

ITDA Paderu works with the Collectorate so that tribal welfare, education, health and livelihood programmes in the scheduled area are aligned with district administration.

Headquarters

Paderu, Alluri Sitharama Raju District, Andhra Pradesh.

Role

District administration, public grievance coordination and inter-department review.

ITDA link

ITDA Paderu is the tribal development agency for the mandals of this district.

For the current District Collector and official circulars, confirm with the Collectorate at Paderu or see Who's Who.
