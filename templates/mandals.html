Mandals of Alluri Sitharama Raju District

The Sub-Division is divided into Mandals. Alluri Sitharamaraju District consists of 11 Mandals. Mandal is headed by Tahsildar.

Tahsildar

The Tahsildar is vested with the same powers and functions of Tahsildars of erstwhile Taluks including magisterial powers.

The Tahsildar heads the Mandal Revenue Office.

1. Public Interface

Provides the interface between the government and public within his jurisdiction.

2. Welfare Measures

Initiates welfare measures within his jurisdiction.

3. Information & Inquiries

Assists higher authorities in collecting information and conducting inquiries.

4. District Administration Feedback

Provides feedback to the district administration that helps in decision-making at higher levels of administration.

Deputy Tahsildar / Superintendent

The Deputy Tahsildar/Superintendent supervises the day-to-day functions of Tahsildar’s office and deals mainly with general administration.

  • Supervises day-to-day office functions
  • Deals with general administration
  • Most files are routed through him
  • Monitors all sections in the Tahsildar’s office

Mandal Revenue Inspector (MRI)

The Mandal Revenue Inspector assists the Tahsildar in conducting inquiries and inspections.

  • Assists Tahsildar in inquiries
  • Assists in inspections
  • Supervises Village Secretaries
  • Inspects crop fields (Azmoish)
  • Writes Sharas / field inspection details in Pahani
  • Collects land revenue
  • Collects non-agricultural land assessment
  • Collects other dues
  • Keeps close watch on villages within jurisdiction
  • Helps maintain law and order

Assistant Statistical Officer (ASO)

The Assistant Statistical Officer (ASO), who is under the overall control of Chief Planning Officer at the District and Directorate of Economics and Statistics at the State Level, maintains data related to rainfall, crops and population.

🌧️ Rainfall

Maintains rainfall-related data.

🌾 Crops

Maintains crop-related data and conducts crop estimate tests.

👥 Population

Maintains population-related data.

Responsibilities

  • Conducts crop estimate tests
  • Inspects crops and submits crop condition details
  • Prepares periodical reports on births and deaths
  • Assists the Tahsildar in livestock census
  • Assists in population census
  • Assists in other government surveys
