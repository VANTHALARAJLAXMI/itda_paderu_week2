Public Services & Citizen Assistance

Summary of public services, certificate guidance, educational admissions, and citizen assistance

1. Ashram School Admissions

Description: Free residential school admission for tribal children.

Eligibility: ST students residing in Agency mandals.

Documents: Caste certificate, Aadhaar, study certificate.

Process: Submit application to Ashram School HM.

Office Placeholder: [School HM / ITDA Education Section]

2. Post-Matric Hostel Admission

Description: Free lodging for tribal college students.

Eligibility: ST students enrolled in recognized colleges.

Documents: College admission slip, caste/income cert, Aadhaar.

Process: Online Jnanabhumi portal or Hostel Warden submission.

Office Placeholder: [Hostel Warden / Tribal Welfare Office Paderu]

3. Caste & Income Certificate Guidance

Description: Certificate guidance for accessing tribal welfare benefits.

Eligibility: Resident tribal citizens.

Documents: Ration card, Aadhaar card, VRO report.

Process: Apply via Meeseva / Grama Sachivalayam.

Office Placeholder: [Tahsildar Office Paderu]

4. RoFR Forest Land Rights Guidance

Description: Traditional forest land title application assistance.

Eligibility: Forest-dwelling ST families cultivating forest land.

Documents: Possession proof, Gram Sabha resolution.

Process: Gram Sabha verification -> DLC approval.

Office Placeholder: [ITDA Survey Wing / Sub-Collector Office]

5. Minimuluru Coffee Plant Saplings Supply

Description: Subsidized coffee saplings and extension guidance.

Eligibility: Registered tribal coffee growers.

Documents: Land patta, Aadhaar, bank passbook.

Process: Registration at Coffee Board Paderu office.

Office Placeholder: [Deputy Director Extension, Coffee Board Paderu]

6. Public Grievance Redressal

Description: Registering citizen complaints regarding public services.

Eligibility: Open to all agency citizens.

Documents: Written grievance / application proof.

Process: Submit at ITDA Spandana / Grievance Cell.

Office Placeholder: [ITDA Grievance Cell, Paderu]
