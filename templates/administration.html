Administrative Setup

District administration at Paderu, from the Collectorate to mandals and Gram Panchayats, with ITDA as the tribal development agency for the full district.

Collectorate

District headquarters at Paderu and general administration of Alluri Sitharama Raju District.

Revenue Division

Paderu revenue division and field revenue administration in the Agency area.

Mandals

Eleven scheduled mandals of the present district, all under ITDA jurisdiction.

Village & Panchayats

Panchayat Raj civic amenities, Gram Panchayat income sources and local administration.
