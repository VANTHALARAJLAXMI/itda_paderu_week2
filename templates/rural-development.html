Rural Development & Village Infrastructure

Rural connectivity, safe drinking water, sanitation, and MGNREGA wage employment in Paderu Agency

Rural Infrastructure & Connectivity

Purpose: Connecting hill habitations with all-weather roads, culverts, and bridges to enable transport for ambulances, buses, and agricultural produce.

Key Projects: Panchayati Raj road construction, tribal village causeways, community halls, and streetlight electrification.

Drinking Water & MGNREGA Employment

Purpose: Providing gravity-based drinking water schemes and guaranteed wage employment for rural tribal households.

Services: Job cards, trench digging, watershed development, check dams, and borewell handpump maintenance.
