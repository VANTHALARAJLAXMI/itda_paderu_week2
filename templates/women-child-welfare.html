Women & Child Welfare

Anganwadi services, maternal nutrition, child protection, and women empowerment initiatives

Anganwadi & Child Nutrition Services

Purpose: Eradicating child malnutrition in remote tribal hamlets through supplementary nutrition meals, growth monitoring, and pre-school non-formal education.

Services: Daily nutritious hot cooked meals, take-home ration (THR), iron-folic acid supplementation, and immunization.

Beneficiaries: Children aged 6 months to 6 years, pregnant women, and lactating mothers.

Where to Approach: Local Anganwadi Centre / CDPO (Child Development Project Officer), Paderu.

Maternal Health & Women Empowerment

Purpose: Assisting tribal women with safe institutional delivery, financial assistance, self-help groups (SHG), and skill development.

Services: Sukanya Samriddhi awareness, SHG bank linkage, sanitary hygiene kits, and vocational training.

Where to Approach: ICDS Office Paderu or DRDA Tribal Women Cell.
