History of ITDA Paderu

The Alluri Sitharama Raju District was formed from the Paderu and Rampachodavaram divisions. The entire district comes under the jurisdiction of ITDA.

🏛

Establishment

On 4 April 2022, Andhra Pradesh reorganised its districts, doubling the total from 13 to 26. The 13 new districts were carved from existing ones to improve administration.

Before 2022, the Paderu Revenue Division belonged to the erstwhile Visakhapatnam district, and the Rampachodavaram Revenue Division belonged to the erstwhile Kakinada district. The new district was named after the revolutionary Alluri Sitarama Raju, known as the Manyam Veerudu (Hero of the Jungle). He led the Rampa Rebellion (1922–1924) against British colonial rule from this region. The administrative headquarters of the district is at Paderu.

Why this name?

The district commemorates Alluri Sitarama Raju, who organised resistance in the Agency hills. ITDA Paderu continues as the tribal development institution for the mandals that remain in the district after later reorganisation.

👥

Reorganisation in 2025

With effect from 31 December 2025, Andhra Pradesh reorganised its districts again, increasing the total from 26 to 28.

The new district of Polavaram, with headquarters at Rampachodavaram, was created by combining 12 mandals from the former Alluri Sitharama Raju District. The present Alluri Sitharama Raju District was formed with 11 mandals and its headquarters at Paderu. The entire district remains under ITDA jurisdiction.

Paderu — the administrative centre

Paderu is the headquarters of the Alluri Sitharama Raju District and the seat of ITDA. The district is one of the north-eastern districts of Andhra Pradesh. It lies between 17°17′ and 18°21′ north and 80°53′ and 82°50′ east.

It is bounded on the north partly by Odisha, on the south by Anakapalli District, on the west by Polavaram District, and on the east by Vizianagaram District.

Physical features

The district presents a distinct geographic division. It consists of the hilly regions of the Eastern Ghats, with an altitude of about 900 metres, dotted by several peaks exceeding 1200 metres. Forest block topping with 1615 metres embraces the mandals of:

  • Ananthagiri
  • Araku Valley
  • Dumbriguda
  • Hukumpeta
  • Munchingput
  • Pedabayalu
  • Paderu
  • G. Madugula
  • Chinthapalli
  • G.K. Veedhi
  • Koyyuru

This page is a reference summary of district formation. For gazette notifications and the current mandal list, confirm with the district administration at Paderu.

The history of ITDA Paderu is closely associated with the Government’s continuing efforts to promote inclusive development, protect tribal interests and improve the quality of life of communities living in the remote and hilly areas of the Eastern Ghats.
