Scholarships & Student Financial Support

Guidance on pre-matric and post-matric scholarship applications for Scheduled Tribe students

Step-by-Step Scholarship Application Process

  1. Step 1 (Document Preparation): Keep your Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Bank Passbook, and latest Marksheet ready.
  2. Step 2 (Online Registration): Register on the state scholarship portal (Jnanabhumi) through your school/college operator.
  3. Step 3 (Biometric Authentication): Complete e-KYC biometric authentication at school or Meeseva centre.
  4. Step 4 (Verification): Application is verified by the Institution Principal and forwarded to the Deputy Director (Tribal Welfare), Paderu.
  5. Step 5 (DBT Disbursement): Scholarship amounts and fee reimbursements are credited directly to the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

Pre-Matric Scholarships (Classes 5 to 10)

Assists day scholars and hostel students with maintenance stipends and book allowances to prevent dropouts.

Post-Matric Scholarships (Colleges & Higher Ed)

Covers tuition fee reimbursement and maintenance allowances for tribal students pursuing Intermediate, ITI, Polytechnic, Degree, Engineering, and Medical courses.
