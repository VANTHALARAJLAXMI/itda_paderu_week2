Forest Conservation & Biodiversity

Protecting the dense Eastern Ghats forest cover, prevention of forest fires, biodiversity conservation, and promoting eco-tourism in Araku and Paderu valleys.

Non-Timber Forest Produce (NTFP / Minor Forest Produce)

Tribal families collect honey, tamarind, adda leaves, gum, and medicinal plants. AP Girijan Cooperative Corporation (GCC) ensures fair procurement prices to protect collectors from exploitation.
