Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common inquiries regarding ITDA Paderu, Super 50, Ashram schools, health camps, and public services

ITDA stands for Integrated Tribal Development Agency. ITDA Paderu is the government body responsible for tribal welfare, education, health, agriculture, coffee cultivation, and infrastructure development in the Scheduled Area of Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR) District, Andhra Pradesh.

ITDA manages Ashram schools (Classes 3–10), post-matric college hostels, pre-matric & post-matric scholarships, computer lab education, and the 'Super 50' intensive coaching program for tribal Class 10 merit students.

Students can apply online through the state Jnanabhumi scholarship portal or consult the scholarship desk officer at the ITDA Paderu office or their respective college/school HM.

The official district health page lists District Hospital Paderu (200 beds), Area Hospital Araku (150 beds), CHC Chintapalli (50 beds) and CHC Munchingiput (30 beds), plus JSY, JSSK, PMSMA, blood bank/storage and SNCU services. This site does not claim a named service for a remote village unless that record is official. Emergency numbers published on this portal include 108, 104 and 112.

Citizens can submit written representations during the weekly Monday Spandana / Grievance Day held at the ITDA Office Paderu premises, or approach concerned Mandal Tahsildar / MPDO offices.

For verified official circulars, officer contact numbers, and live scheme notifications, visit the official ASR District portal or the ITDA office complex at Paderu town.
